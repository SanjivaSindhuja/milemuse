# Interstate packs: offline stories for every US interstate, shown as packs without crowding Explore

Date: 2026-11-12. Status: for review. Builds on `2026-11-10-national-park-packs-design.md`. Everything there applies
here unless this doc says otherwise: manifest v2, diff sync, content-addressed audio, Essentials and Full tiers, free
samples, refresh by hand, and pack-first playback.

## Why

- **Park packs proved the model.** Stories are written once, shared, played offline, and cost cents per listener.
- **Interstates are the longest drives and the worst dead zones.** Examples: I-80 in Nevada and Wyoming, I-90 in
  Montana, I-10 in west Texas, I-15 in Utah and Nevada, I-40 in Arizona and New Mexico.
- **They are also the most repeated drives.** Commuters use the same stretches daily: the owner drives I-5 and I-405.

The owner asked for content for all the interstates, generated by agents, sold and shown as packs, with a UX that
scales instead of overloading Explore.

## Goals / non-goals / anti-goals

**Goals**

- **Coverage.** Every primary (2-digit) interstate, about 70 routes and an estimated ~33,000 miles (~53,000 km).
- **One pack per highway per state**, for example "I-90 · Montana". A state stretch over ~500 km is split at a metro
  area, for example "I-10 · Texas: El Paso – San Antonio".
- **The same tiers as parks:**
  - Essentials: one story per ~3 km stretch, plus landmark stops.
  - Full: every topic with material on that stretch.
- **Writing by Claude Code agents** from exported briefs, with no Bedrock cost, as for the park build (26.5k stories).
  The pipeline then voices them (Polly) and publishes them.
- **Listener memory.** Pack items become notes. A heard item isn't played again for 30 days. Live narration fills in
  after that.
- **A UX that scales to 300+ packs:**
  - Downloads attach to routes.
  - One Packs library.
  - Explore gets one entry point, not shelves.

**Non-goals (v1)**

- 3-digit spurs and loops, except I-405 (WA) in the pilot (the owner's commute). Cities have signal, so live
  narration covers them.
- US highways and state routes.
- Business details in packs (gas, food, hours): they go stale. Exit lines stay live.
- Other voices, tones, levels or languages. One version, as for parks.

**Anti-goals**

- A single download over ~150 MB.
- Highway shelves crowding Explore.
- A stale business fact ("open 24 hours", "new").
- Retelling what the listener already heard, live or from a pack.
- Anything personal in pack content.

## Data model and storage

**Hierarchy:** Highway (`I-90`) → Segment (one state, or a section of one) → Stretch (~3 km) → Items.

**Item kinds:**

- `story`: a stretch told from one topic, direction-neutral.
- `stop`: a landmark within 400 m. Its side is decided on the phone at play time.
- `overview`: a segment-level story per topic, for gaps.
- Lead-in clips: shared with parks.

**Stores:** DynamoDB `HW#<segment>` items, shaped like parks' `NP#`. S3 at `hw/<segment>/{e,f,s}/<hash>.mp3`, plus
manifests. The same CloudFront signed cookie protects Full.

## Pipeline (reuses ParkPrime)

1. **Roads.** OpenStreetMap route relations (`network=US:I`, `ref`), clipped to each state.
   - Both carriageways merge into one centre line within ~50 m.
   - Stretch ids stay stable while the geometry doesn't change.
2. **Facts per stretch.**
   - Wikipedia landmarks within ~3 km.
   - The towns and counties it passes, told section by section as in listener memory (`articleSections`).
   - Land beside the road, and Wikidata numbers.
   - Each item gets a `factsHash`.
3. **Drafts.**
   - `hw-drafts.mjs export <segment>` writes briefs (the park prompt plus interstate rules).
   - Spawned agents write `[{ id, factsHash, text, themes }]`.
   - `load` checks each draft against the lint: banned phrases, direction-neutral, no business, hours, closures or
     "currently".
4. **Voice and publish.** `HighwayPrime`, the ParkPrime state machine with a highway source. Pilot segments wait on a
   review flag.
5. **Refresh.** By hand only, diff-only, as for parks.

## Agents (the generation)

- After export, writer agents run in parallel, about 8 at a time. Each owns a few segments' batches and returns
  drafts.
- Drafts are loaded and voiced batch by batch.
- A reviewer agent samples about 20 items per pilot segment. The owner listens to samples before a pilot segment
  publishes.
- A failed or rejected batch is re-exported and rewritten. Nothing half-written publishes.

## Playback (phone)

- **Pack-first.** `parkplay.ts` becomes pack playback. Inside a downloaded segment's corridor (within ~60 m of its
  line), its items play first, offline or online, using the same stretch, heading and loop logic as parks.
- **Live fills in.** Listener memory skips heard items, so live narration fills whatever is left.
- **Notes.** Pack items become notes with a new note kind, `pack`. The server's notes schema adds it.

## UX that scales (owner: don't overload Explore)

1. **On a planned trip, the route decides.** The trip card shows "Offline stories for this route: I-90 WA, I-90 MT ·
   140 MB · Download". It downloads only the segments the route uses: Essentials by default, Full with Plus.
   - On Wi-Fi by default.
   - It downloads by itself on Wi-Fi for a trip starting within 24 h (setting, on).
2. **One Packs library.** Reached from Explore through one "Offline packs" row, and from You > Downloads.
   - Tabs: Parks | Highways.
   - Highways has a search ("I-90", "Montana") and a list of highways.
   - A highway page shows its states, each with sizes, tiers, downloaded/update and Remove.
3. **Commute.** Once a commute is detected, the library shows one card: "Your commute: I-5 and I-405 · 30 MB ·
   Download".
4. **Explore** keeps its National Parks shelf, plus the one "Offline packs" row. No highway shelves.
5. **Car.** Android Auto and CarPlay get a Packs folder, as for Parks.

## Pricing (owner decision)

My default: Essentials free, Full included in Plus. A per-segment unlock comes later, once store purchases are real
(as for parks).

## Pilot, then all

**Pilot corridors:**

- I-5: WA, OR, northern CA.
- I-405: WA.
- I-90: WA, ID, MT.
- I-80: NV, WY.
- I-15: UT, NV.

These cover dead zones and the owner's commute.

**Gates before the rest:**

- Build cost at most $5 per segment.
- Essentials at most 40 MB per segment.
- The owner samples 20 items per segment.
- A drive test on the owner's commute.

## Owner decisions (to confirm)

1. Scope: primaries plus I-405 for the pilot, not every 3-digit route.
2. Pricing: Essentials free, Full in Plus.
3. Pilot the 5 corridors first, then all primaries.
4. UX: route-attached downloads plus one Packs library (mockups on request).
