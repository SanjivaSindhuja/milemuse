# Ambience: weather, time and season in stories; background music; a voice that moves

Date: 2026-11-06. Status: approved for autonomous build ("come up with the design and implement autonomously").

## Why

The guide should feel like a friend in the passenger seat who knows *this* moment: a wet November evening on I-5
is not a sunny July noon. The LLM is there to use that context well. And the drive should sound cinematic:
pleasant music under the voice, and the voice moving to where the thing it talks about is.

## Goals

1. **Moment-aware stories.** Every "Just drive" story knows the local day, date and time, the time of day
   (morning, afternoon, evening, night, sunset soon), the season (by hemisphere) and the weather now
   (sky, rain/snow, temperature, wind). The guide weaves it in naturally where it fits what the driver sees:
   rain on the windshield, early dark in November, fall colour, the season's wildlife and farm work.
2. **Background music.** Soft, pleasant, original (generated by us, so no copyright at all). Three moods picked
   by the moment: *bright* (clear or cloudy daytime), *calm* (night, fog), *cozy* (rain, snow, storm).
   Quiet under the voice, a little fuller between stories. On by default, a switch in settings.
3. **A voice that moves.** A sentence that says "on your left" comes from the left, "on your right" from the
   right; "behind you / back there / just passed" sounds behind (darker, a little quieter); everything else centre.
4. **Works everywhere the app plays today:** car speakers through CarPlay or Bluetooth, the phone, AirPods,
   offline downloads, the lock screen.

## Non-goals / anti-goals

- Not Dolby Atmos: an app reaches the car through CarPlay or Bluetooth, which carry stereo. Left/right is
  the most a car can get from us; "behind" is a cue, not true rear placement.
- Never hard-pan: a driver must hear every word from either seat. Pan at most ~60% to a side.
- No invented events: weather and season are general knowledge about the region, never "the festival this weekend".
- Music never competes with the voice (stays ~18 dB under it while the guide talks).

## Design

### 1. Moment (server, `core/src/moment.mjs`)
- Open-Meteo forecast API (free, no key; `current` weather + `daily` sunrise/sunset, `timezone=auto` gives local
  time). Cached in the Lambda per ~10 km cell for 15 minutes. A failure only leaves the moment out.
  ponytail: Open-Meteo's free tier is for non-commercial use; buy its API plan (or switch provider) before launch.
- `{ when: "Friday 6 November, 6:45 pm", daypart, season, sky, weather: "overcast, 12°C (54°F), breezy",
  sunset, mood }`. WMO weather codes map to sky: clear, cloudy, fog, rain, snow, storm.
- Prompt: a MOMENT line with the facts, and an instruction to bring in the time of day, the season and the
  weather where they fit what the driver sees, as general knowledge.
- Story library key gains the moment bucket (season, daypart, sky) so a rainy-night story is never replayed on
  a sunny morning; key version v4. Offline (downloaded) stories get the season only: they play later.
- The story carries `mood` for the music.

### 2. Voice placement (server, `core/src/spatial.mjs`)
- Polly parts (MP3) are decoded (mpg123-decoder, wasm, MIT), each sentence's time estimated from its share of
  its part's characters, and its place found from what it says (left / right / behind / centre).
- Mono → stereo with an equal-power pan, 0.4 s glides between places; behind = centre, -3 dB, low-passed.
- Loudness: gated speech level brought to about -16 LUFS with a soft peak limiter at -1 dBFS (replaces the
  fixed MP3 gain step); encoded stereo 64 kbps (wasm-media-encoders: LAME in WebAssembly, server-side only).
- Decode or encode failing falls back to today's mono MP3 (with the fixed gain), so a story is never lost.
- Duration comes from the sample count, not the byte count.

### 3. Music (app)
- Three ~3 minute seamless loops, made by a script in the app repo (`scripts/music.mjs`: soft pads, a slow
  chord progression, sparse pentatonic plucks, reverb), encoded to AAC and bundled as assets (~1.5 MB each).
- A second expo-audio player loops the bed. Volume glides: 0.08 while a story plays, 0.25 between stories,
  fades out when the drive ends. The mood follows each story's `mood`.
- Setting "Background music" (on by default) in You > Listening.

## Left to the builder
- Exact pan amount, glide time, music volumes and synthesis voicing (tune by ear and measurement).
- Planned trips (pipeline scripts) get the moment later: their stories are written at plan time for a departure
  that may be hours away.
